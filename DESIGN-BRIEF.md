## 4. Design direction: "Pine & Ember"

**Mood:** late afternoon under the pines going into a bonfire night. Warm timber, dappled shade, linen, and the glow of a fire. Rustic and well looked after, not glossy.

### Palette (from the logo and the photos)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Pine | `#1E3B2F` | header on scroll, footer, dark sections |
| Moss | `#5F6F3A` | eyebrows, icons, small accents (the logo's olive) |
| Linen | `#F5EFE3` | main background |
| Sand | `#E8DCC4` | alternate section background, cards |
| Bark | `#3A2A1E` | body text |
| Mango gold | `#D6A13C` | rules, stars, highlights (the logo's gold) |
| Ember | `#B4532A` | primary CTA "Message us" / "Reserve" (the bonfire) |

**Why:** the brand is green, but an all-green site would merge into the photos. Linen gives the timber shots space to breathe, pine anchors the page, and one warm ember color makes the booking actions stand out.

### Type
- **Display: Fraunces** (Google Fonts, variable). A soft, slightly old-style serif that feels hand-made and warm, like the log cabin. Its italic replaces Aria's script ("*Welcome to*", "*Bonfire nights*").
- **Body and UI: Manrope.** Clean and friendly, easy to read on phones. Eyebrows set uppercase with wide letter-spacing.
- Scale (fluid): H1 44→84px · H2 32→52px · H3 20→26px · body 16–17px, line-height 1.65.

### Layout and sections (mapped to Aria)
1. **Header:** wordmark on the left. Nav: Stay · Grounds · Explore · Gallery · Reviews · Location. "Message us" (ember) on the right, and a hamburger on mobile. Transparent over the hero, then pine once you scroll.
2. **Hero:** full-bleed Balai photo (main villa veranda, or the rice-field and mountain view). Eyebrow "— San Antonio · Zambales —", H1 "Where stress goes to completely fade away". Two CTAs: *Message us on Messenger* and *Call 0942 972 7377*.
3. **Plan-your-stay bar:** stands in for Aria's booking bar. Check-in, check-out, guests and a "Ask for rates" button that opens Messenger. No booking engine. The bar also shows ★ 4.89 Airbnb · Superhost.
4. **Welcome / the story:** italic "Welcome to", the H2 "Balai Taltalon", the motto "Escape. Relax. Reconnect." and a short story built from their own description. Key facts row: 1 hectare · up to 15 guests · check-in 2 PM · pet-friendly.
5. **The Log Cabin & Guest Wing:** two full-bleed split features with overlapping cards showing beds, baths and capacity, plus a combined "Whole resort, up to 15 guests" note.
6. **Open kitchen & alfresco:** Aria's "Dining" slot. Kitchen, pool-side table, grill, karaoke.
7. **The Grounds** (Aria's Spa & Leisure): image cards for Plunge pool · Pine grove · Bonfire nights · Mango shade & hammocks · Fishponds · Courts & bikes.
8. **Explore nearby:** Pundaquit Beach, Anawangin and Nagsasa coves, Capones and Camara islands, Subic. Drive times only where they're confirmed.
9. **Ways to stay** (Aria's Special Offers): Whole resort · Main villa · Events and team building (from their bio). Each card says "Ask us for rates".
10. **Gallery:** masonry grid with a lightbox.
11. **Guest reviews:** real Airbnb quotes credited by first name and platform (Kristine, Ange, Rhoda, Lyka, Hazel, Samantha…) and the 4.89 score.
12. **Location:** embedded map, the address and the drive times.
13. **Contact / inquiry form:** see the open question below.
14. **Footer:** pine band with the round logo badge, contact details, links to Facebook, Airbnb and Booking.com, and the house-rules summary (check-in and check-out times).
15. Floating **Messenger button** at bottom right.

### Motion and performance
Subtle fade-and-rise reveals, a slow Ken Burns zoom on the hero only, and support for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Images use `<picture>` with WebP and a JPG fallback at 640, 1280 and 1920 widths, and everything below the fold lazy-loads. Fonts load with `display=swap`. No frameworks. Target: under about 1.2 MB on first load for mobile.

### Imagery plan
- **Primary:** Balai's own Airbnb listing photos (about 20 found, none with text on them) and their own Facebook photos (bonfire, cabana, pool). I'll clean these up: exposure, white balance, light denoising, sharpening, and upscaling where needed. I'll leave out the Facebook promo posters and any image labelled AI.
- **Gaps:** free-license Unsplash or Pexels photos for the nearby coves and beaches, all logged in `assets/SOURCES.md`.
