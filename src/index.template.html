Welcome to

Balai Taltalon

Escape. Relax. Reconnect.

Out past the rice fields of Brgy. Luna, a gravel lane opens onto one private hectare of shade: tall pines, old mango trees, flowering gardens and three quiet fishponds, all under the Zambales mountains.

At the centre is a hand-built log cabin with warm timber floors, rattan furniture and a veranda that catches the breeze. Days drift between the plunge pool, the hammocks and the grill. At night everyone gathers around the bonfire. You wake to birdsong. Families come to reconnect, barkadas come for the karaoke, and teams come to slow down together.
